# Heriot-Watt/Edinburgh manuscript — claim-level source checks

Sources examined 21 November 2026. Each locator supports the particular model or prior-work statement below.

| Ref. | Manuscript claim | Source and locator |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A more general footstep planner optimizes placements over convex terrain regions. | [Deits & Tedrake, full MIT author paper](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/robotics-center/public_papers/Deits14a.pdf), Eqs. (1)–(2) assign each footstep to exactly one safe convex region. The subsequent reachability and orientation constraints act jointly on neighboring poses. Their reachability and rotation are beyond the six-row graph here. |
| 2 | SL1M addresses combinations of potential contact surfaces under reachability and quasi-static constraints. | [Tonneau et al., full Edinburgh manuscript](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/files/197023309/SL1M_TONNEAU_DOA31012020_AFV.pdf), static-balance constraint requiring the center of mass to project inside the support polygon and subsection “Relative foot position constraints” approximating feasible reach between feet. Their whole-body reach is outside the present model. |
| 3 | Perception can yield foothold constraints in nonlinear locomotion control. | [Grandia et al., full author paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.08373), terrain-processing diagram and Eq. (21) construct convex foothold constraints from the perceived elevation map. Figs. 15–16 show ANYmal hardware experiments. Their results are not tests of the present planner. |
| 4 | The Kinect lateral edge error model supplies σ at a given image range and incidence. | [Nguyen, Izadi & Lovell, author paper](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~nguyen/papers/conferences/Nguyen2012-ModelingKinectSensorNoise.pdf), §2.3.1 Eqs. (1)–(2), Fig. 6 range, apparatus paragraph reporting 585-pixel focal length, Fig. 5 measured noise distributions. The measured scale enters sensor_planner.py. The paper did not measure this study’s terrain maps or register depth-camera errors into a quadruped terrain frame. |
| 5 | A fixed risk allocation plus the union bound yields a conservative path constraint. | [Blackmore, Ono & Williams, MIT accepted paper](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/1d515760-8c7b-409e-8b6b-a9addc3f5cc4), Eqs. (24)–(25) and fixed equal allocation in Problem 5, Eq. (46). Their vehicle planner has different states and dynamics. We implemented the 12-edge finite-graph comparison. |
| 6 | A real robot study observed 2.5 cm of short-term map drift after roughly 2 m of motion. | [Villarreal et al., author manuscript](https://iit-dlslab.github.io/papers/villarreal19ral.pdf), “Uncertainty Margin” subsection; the same passage separately reports 3 cm of foothold-prediction uncertainty. The 25 mm scale enters the sensitivity test. Symmetric left/right allocation, lateral direction, fixed magnitude and whole-map persistence are study assumptions. |
