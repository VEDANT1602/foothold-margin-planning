# Primary source locations

1. [CERBERUS Finals release, ETH Zürich](https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000552248). The original sensor bag and estimate/registered trajectory files are available under `anymal_1`. The [maintainers’ Final Event README](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/cerberus_darpa_subt_datasets#final-event), under “Trajectories per robot,” distinguishes in-run CompSLAM from post-processed estimates. Under “Ground-Truth,” it specifies individual VLP-16 registration to the DARPA course map. The survey and sensor data are separate acquisitions, while the registered trajectory uses that survey.
2. [DARPA downsampled survey](https://github.com/subtchallenge/systems_finals_ground_truth/tree/8276a16155eddf966e4458e40054bc53a31c8ac5), “Repo Contents,” file `data/systems_final.pcd`. Its header specifies 1,000,000 xyz points.
3. [Tranzatto et al., Science Robotics](https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.abp9742). CERBERUS sensors and deployment at the finals.
4. [VLP-16 manual](https://data.ouster.io/downloads/velodyne/user-manual/vlp-16-user-manual-revf.pdf), “Data Packet Format,” beam order and azimuth interpolation. This replay applies nominal calibration.
5. [Villarreal et al., authors’ full-text PDF, page 5](https://iit-dlslab.github.io/papers/villarreal19ral.pdf#page=5). The lower-left column in Sec. IV-C(c), **Uncertainty Margin**, states 3 cm of nominal-foothold uncertainty linked to trunk pose estimation and separately 2.5 cm of short-term map drift after about 2 m of travel. This is article page 4 (the cover makes it PDF page 5). The preceding Sec. IV-A reports an approximately 3-cm average landing-prediction error during a preliminary flat-ground trot; that is a separate experiment. None of these three quantities is the roughness cutoff used here. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2019.2899434).
